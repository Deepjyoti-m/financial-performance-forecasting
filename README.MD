# Financial Performance & Forecasting Analytics


## 📌 Business Context
Organizations rely on timely and accurate financial insights to evaluate performance, manage profitability, and plan future growth.  
This project simulates an **FP&A-style financial analysis**, focusing on historical performance, key financial KPIs, and short-term revenue forecasting to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze historical financial performance
- Track key revenue and profitability KPIs
- Identify trends and seasonality
- Forecast future revenue
- Present insights through executive-level dashboards

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The dataset represents monthly financial performance data, including:
- Revenue
- Costs and expenses
- Gross profit
- Operating profit
- Product / business unit breakdown
- Time-based trends

> Data has been cleaned, transformed, and modeled to reflect real-world financial reporting structures.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs Analyzed
- Total Revenue
- Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth
- Gross Margin
- Operating Margin
- Revenue Contribution by Product / Segment
- Forecast vs Actual Performance

---

## 🛠️ Analytical Approach

### 1️⃣ Data Preparation
- Cleaned and standardized raw financial data
- Created derived metrics such as margins and growth rates
- Structured data for analysis and reporting

### 2️⃣ SQL-Based KPI Analysis
- Aggregated monthly financial performance
- Calculated core FP&A metrics
- Built reusable queries for reporting

### 3️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)
- Revenue and margin trend analysis
- Seasonality identification
- Variance and performance drivers

### 4️⃣ Revenue Forecasting
- Built a time-series forecasting model
- Compared historical actuals vs predicted values
- Evaluated forecast accuracy and assumptions

### 5️⃣ Executive Dashboard (Power BI)
- High-level KPI overview
- Profitability analysis
- Forecast visualization for management review

---

## 📊 Tools & Technologies
- **Python** – pandas, numpy, matplotlib, forecasting models
- **SQL** – KPI aggregation and financial metrics
- **Power BI** – interactive executive dashboards
- **Excel** – financial validation and reconciliation

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Revenue demonstrates clear seasonal patterns across periods
- Certain products / segments drive a disproportionate share of profitability
- Margin trends highlight opportunities for cost optimization
- Forecast indicates steady short-term growth under current assumptions

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus investment on high-margin products or segments
- Use forecast outputs to support budgeting and capacity planning
- Monitor margin compression risks through ongoing KPI tracking
- Automate recurring financial reporting for faster decision-making
